Helpful ownership tools

Plan the next step.

These tools run in your browser and use information you enter. Estimates are illustrative and do not replace a lender, dealer quote or professional inspection.

LOAN PLANNER

Estimate a monthly payment.

Select an India-market car, then use a current dealer quote and lender rate.

Model-year windows are indicative. EMI is an estimate; lender terms, fees and final on-road price vary.
MILEAGE PLANNER

See your running cost.

Choose the car and enter its claimed or measured efficiency and your local fuel price.

Uses the values you enter. It does not fetch claimed mileage or live fuel prices.
RESALE MARKET GUIDE

Compare real asking prices.

Choose the India-market car and open current used-car listings from established marketplaces.

Spinny opens its model-specific used-car page directly. Other marketplace links search the entered make, model, year, city and odometer. Velocitrak does not scrape listings or claim ads match your exact vehicle; check each listing’s photos and details.
PRIVATE SERVICE NOTES

Keep a service record.

Add a reminder of work you choose to record. Saved only in this browser on this device.

Personal notes only; Velocitrak does not retrieve or verify service history.

Local alerts work while the site is open. The due date is checked in this browser while the site is open. No cloud or closed-site push alerts are connected.

PRIVATE LISTING DRAFT
